# REAL-SCENE ANIME STAMP WINDOW｜实景动漫邮票窗

Use the image-generation/editing capability to process every uploaded travel photo independently. Output one finished artwork per source photo; never combine multiple photos into one image.

## Non-negotiable composition

- Fill the entire canvas with the complete original photograph. Preserve the subject's identity, count, pose, real scale, spatial position, viewing angle, natural light, environmental relationships, and photographic texture.
- At the exact original location of the core subject, cut a flat stamp window with a complete perforated edge. The window must cover the subject's original main occupancy; do not move it to a convenient layout position.
- Inside the window, redraw the same subject and only a small amount of its immediately corresponding local environment as a restrained retro animation editorial illustration. Outside the window, keep the photograph clear, complete, and recognizably photographic.
- The inside and outside must depict the same instant: same subject count, center, scale, pose, orientation, perspective, and occlusion. Never show both a complete photographed subject and a complete illustrated duplicate.
- Ground and structural continuities must meet naturally across the perforation: feet, wheels, hull waterline, road, rail, steps, railing, horizon, shadows, and other contact lines. Never shift, enlarge, mirror, or replace the subject.
- The stamp window is a flat visual region attached to the photo surface. It must have no thickness, cast shadow, curl, lift, tilt, floating-card effect, sticker effect, or physical postcard appearance.

## Stamp geometry

Choose a stable ratio from the subject orientation:

- Standing people, front-facing vehicles, towers, and vertical street scenes: portrait 3:4.
- Horizontal vehicles, boats, stalls, and waterfront scenes: landscape 4:3.
- Only clearly elongated subjects may use landscape 5:3.
- Never use arbitrary square, ultra-narrow portrait, or excessively panoramic proportions.

The four edges must have continuous, crisp, evenly spaced, uniform complete perforations. Keep the inner illustration panel evenly inset with equal paper margins on all sides. Do not use a plain white border, dashed line, rounded UI-card border, or incomplete teeth.

## Illustration language inside the window

- Use loose ink lines, retro animation cel-style flat fills, restrained old-print grain, and subtle paper texture.
- Extract and simplify the source colors into 3–5 colors while retaining the subject's most identifying colors.
- Remove complex photographic detail. Keep only the subject silhouette, action, defining structures, and necessary environmental cues.
- Reserve approximately 35–60% of the interior for calm color fields or visual quiet. Do not draw a complete, crowded background.
- The illustration must look like a small area of reality suddenly switching to animation, not an additional image pasted over the photograph and not a full-scene anime conversion.

## Interior text

Generate only a small amount of real, legible English based on the visible place, subject, and scene:

1. One readable 1–3 word main title.
2. One location/number line, for example `LOCATION / SCENE NOTE 01`.
3. One short, natural observation sentence.

Place text only in a low-information area inside the stamp window. Use lettering consistent with the illustration—retro animation title lettering, lightly ink-depleted serif, or loose hand lettering. The title must remain readable in a chat thumbnail. Never cover faces, joints, actions, vehicles, tracks, ground lines, or core objects. Do not invent uncertain place names; use a generic location line when the location is not reliable.

## Reject or regenerate if any of these appear

- Upper/lower or left/right photo-versus-illustration split, multi-photo collage, full-image anime redraw, or ordinary global filter.
- Stamp moved away from the source subject, subject enlarged into a new close-up, changed subject count, duplicated subject, changed scale/pose/perspective, or broken ground/rail/horizon continuity.
- Missing or uneven perforations, plain frame, dashed border, rounded card, sticker, floating paper, paper thickness, curl, tilt, or cast projection.
- Complete or crowded interior background, fine polished Japanese anime rendering, unrelated scenery, or illustration that fails to cover the original subject's same-position occupancy.
- Author name, signature, logo, URL, QR code, watermark, gibberish, or unreadable fake text.

## Final quality bar

The result should read immediately as one untouched travel photograph containing one precisely aligned flat perforated stamp window. The photograph remains the dominant reality; the window is a selective, calm, source-derived retro animation intervention that preserves the original scene's spatial truth.
